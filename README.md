## 👋 About me

I build end-to-end data work in **Python and SQL**: ETL pipelines that pull data out of the systems a business already runs, data-quality checks, star-schema warehouses, and the dashboards and forecasts managers actually use. Every tool is built once and fitted to each sector, starting with hospitality.

My background is a **Master's in Data Analytics** (2026) and an **MSc in Physics** (cosmology), so I am at home with both the mathematics and the business question behind a number. I also know hotel operations from the floor up, which is why my first BI toolkit is for hotels.
